Most pharmacy jobs are counting. This one is preparing.

If you trained to compound and have not touched a balance since, this is the bench where that changes.

See open roles

What is actually different about working here

No perks list. Three things that are true about the work itself.

You compound, daily

01

Preparation is the core of the job, not an occasional exception squeezed between dispensing runs.

You speak to prescribers

02

When a formulation is ambiguous you make the call yourself. Clinical judgement is part of the role, not escalated away from it.

Your name is on it

03

A small bench means the work is attributable. That is a responsibility and it is also the reason the work is worth doing.

One branch, no rotation

04

You work with the same two pharmacists and the same patients, and you learn both properly.
